# Changelog

Newest version first. The top `## x.y.z` heading = `Plugin.ModVersion` = `manifest.json` `version_number` (integration tests check it).
Write "not tested in game" for anything that was not run in game.

## 1.0.0
- First release.
- Fluffy Slippers (utility slot, armor, frost resistance) with a little plush troll on each foot.
- Troll taming with the slippers, more trolls spawning 200 - 500 m from your bed, tamed trolls that farm, carry a backpack and build depots
  near your bed, defend you, wait 15 m from the bed and never swing within 15 m of it.
- Troll Mascot costume (head, suit, trousers) and the Troll Club; set bonus Steve The Troll.
- F6 menu: Slippers, Costume, Troll work and Trolls tabs; map pins for your trolls.
